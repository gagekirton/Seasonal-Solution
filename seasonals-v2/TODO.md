# Project outcomes

- [x] **Professional responsive public website.** Remake the entire front end as a polished, accessible, mobile-friendly Winnipeg service-business website with clear navigation, readable page content, keyboard support, strong contrast, and large mobile tap targets. Present Seasonal Solutions and verified business details professionally; provide prominent phone, email, and quote calls to action.

- [x] **Home and service-page content.** Create a conversion-focused home page and detailed, indexable pages for handyman services, landscaping and lawn care, painting, home renovations, and seasonal maintenance. Each service page explains its scope without inventing credentials or guarantees, links to related services, and provides a quote prompt.

- [x] **Useful local service-area coverage.** Provide indexable Winnipeg service-area content for the neighborhoods and nearby communities named by the business. Use useful, distinct location-aware copy and avoid thin/duplicative doorway pages or unsupported location claims.

- [x] **Quote capture and CRM customer identity.** Keep the quote form accessible and store each project request as its own record. Generate a stable, non-sequential customer ID server-side, attach each request to the customer profile using normalized email, and backfill existing quote records without deleting their original contents. Capture landing path and optional UTM source, medium and campaign. Preserve clear success and error states, server-side validation, spam controls, timestamps and no-store private API responses.

- [x] **Customer history and business-value metrics.** Provide a private searchable customer list and profile with stable customer ID, contact details, linked inquiry and service history, first/last contact and returning-customer status. Track service jobs, quotes, accepted/booked amounts, status and dates, then record actual payments and refunds. Show lifetime collected value as payments less refunds and booked value separately. Never label a quote as revenue, infer profit or report a predicted LTV without verified inputs.

- [x] **Follow-up workflow and permission boundaries.** Provide owner-managed, due-date follow-up tasks linked to customers and optionally to a lead or job, with open/completed/cancelled states and an overdue view. Tasks are internal reminders only; do not send or schedule email, SMS or marketing campaigns. Seasonal/promotional follow-up requires recorded marketing opt-in.

- [x] **Separate, evidence-backed marketing consent.** Keep consent to respond to the requested project separate from an optional, unchecked email-marketing opt-in. Record opt-in timestamp, source and wording version; support recording opt-out and never infer permission from an inquiry, a prior purchase or public contact information. Do not send messages through this implementation.

- [x] **Private CRM access and data minimization.** Enforce the configured owner OpenID or explicit admin role server-side for every customer, lead, job, payment and follow-up endpoint. Do not derive administrator privileges from the public business email. Keep private responses out of public HTML, search metadata, sitemap and shared cache; do not store customer payment-card data or request IP addresses.

- [x] **Local search and social metadata.** Implement unique title and description, canonical URL, Open Graph and social preview metadata, semantic headings, crawlable internal links, and meaningful initial HTML for public pages; include a sitemap, robots policy, and accurate service-business structured data using the verified phone and email. Do not use the unverified 123 Main Street address or fabricate credentials, testimonials, ratings, or business claims.

- [x] **Legacy route continuity and content consistency.** Reuse a shared content architecture so service, location, contact, and metadata details remain consistent. Preserve useful existing page paths or provide appropriate redirects/remaps for the former about, work/gallery, testimonials, blog, contact, privacy, and terms routes without creating fake work samples, reviews, or editorial credentials.

- [x] **Backend and persistence baseline.** Keep the React / Express / tRPC / Drizzle architecture and managed database; add the minimum additive application schema and backend procedures required to persist and review leads without damaging existing user/account data.

- [x] **Consistent server/client hydration.** Fix the reported painting-page hydration mismatch. Development SSR and browser JSX use the same Vite transformation pipeline, including editor location attributes; production bundles exclude editor-only attributes on both sides. The painting page has no attribute mismatches or fresh hydration warnings, and public/CRM pages retain meaningful initial HTML and appropriate status codes.

- [x] **Site review delivered.** Review the site for things to improve, refine or change; record prioritized findings with evidence, implement the owner-independent fixes, and list the items that need owner input (confirmed job list, real photos/reviews/Google Business Profile, response-time promise, travel limits) without inventing claims.

- [x] **Unified owner CRM dashboard I can access.** One private `/admin` dashboard, restricted server-side to the configured owner OpenID or admin role, with navigation to Overview, Leads, Customers, Follow-ups, Content, Payments and Integrations; works on desktop and mobile; admin code is not loaded by public pages; all admin pages are noindex and excluded from the sitemap.

- [x] **Manage my stats.** Overview shows, for 7/30/90/365-day ranges with previous-period comparison: page views, visits, leads, lead conversion rate, new customers, jobs booked, booked value, collected value (online vs manual), open and overdue follow-ups; daily views/visits/leads chart; leads by service and source; top pages; traffic sources; revenue by month; lead pipeline. First-party stats store no IP, user agent or visitor ID, skip bots, admin/pay paths and the owner's own devices, and are disclosed in the privacy policy.

- [x] **Manage my leads.** Lead inbox supports search, status and service filters, a needs-response queue, quick mark-contacted, statuses new/contacted/quoted/won/lost/closed, a private internal note, and links to customer profiles; existing customer, job, payment, follow-up and consent behavior is preserved. Each new quote request sends an owner notification without blocking or failing the customer's submission.

- [x] **Post content.** Owner can create, edit, preview, publish, unpublish and delete posts (title, slug, category tips/project/news, related service, area, excerpt, Markdown body, cover image upload with alt text, SEO title/description). Published posts appear at server-rendered `/blog` and `/blog/:slug` with unique metadata, BlogPosting structured data and canonical URLs, are added to `/sitemap.xml`, and surface on related home/service pages; drafts and unknown slugs return 404. Raw HTML in Markdown is not rendered; uploads are owner-only images ≤8 MB.

- [ ] **Stripe integration.** Platform Stripe sandbox enabled. Owner creates a CAD payment request from a job and gets a durable private pay link; the customer pays through Stripe Checkout (card); the signed webhook verifies raw bytes, deduplicates events, and records exactly one Stripe ledger payment on the job, marks the request paid and alerts the owner; failed attempts are noted; requests can be cancelled; `/admin/payments` lists requests and statuses. No card data is stored by the site. Test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 works in Preview.

- [ ] **QuickBooks integration.** Integrations page shows not-configured / connected / needs-reconnect state, the exact redirect URI to register, sandbox/production choice, connect and disconnect. OAuth uses single-use expiring state; tokens are encrypted at rest and refreshed safely. Owner can sync a customer, create an invoice for a job using a chosen service item (and optional tax code), and push recorded payments to that invoice manually or automatically (including Stripe payments); each action is logged with clear errors. Credentials are requested only when the owner has an Intuit developer app.

## Evidence and handoff limits

The dashboard implementation is complete and the site review is delivered in `SITE-REVIEW.md`; practical access, publishing, accounting and retention instructions are in `CRM-GUIDE.md`. TypeScript checks and the production build pass. The latest isolated suite covers CRM authorization/customer transactions, SSR hydration, content payload/byte safety, Winnipeg day boundaries, Stripe checkout/receipt behavior and stable QuickBooks retry IDs. All tested writes use doubles, not the shared database.

The read-only integration audit's concrete defects were corrected: existing-post update payloads, mobile sidebar spacing/labels, acceptance-date booking metrics, manual payment auto-push, refund-channel attribution, UTF-8 body capacity, discounted paid confirmations and serialized/idempotent QuickBooks creates. Cross-site browser mutations to private dashboard APIs are rejected. Public pages, dynamic sitemap and manifest serve correctly; anonymous private APIs deny access and remain no-store.

Additive migrations through the nullable first-booking timestamp are applied. No historical booking dates are inferred; jobs without a known first acceptance date are excluded from period booking statistics. No valid customer inquiry, job, payment or published post was written for testing.

**Stripe remains open for end-to-end validation:** its sandbox and current Preview webhook are configured, but the sandbox is unclaimed and the actual test-card Checkout/webhook round trip was not exercised. Unit tests do not prove this final acceptance clause. Live keys and live transactions are not configured or validated.

**QuickBooks remains open for account configuration and end-to-end validation:** no Intuit Client ID/Secret or company authorization is present. Create/link endpoints, encrypted tokens, single-use state, serialized refresh/create, stable request IDs and manual/online post-commit sync are implemented. Authorized sandbox company tests are still needed. This is not two-way synchronization or a durable retry queue; later invoice edits/taxes/refunds require accounting review.

The configured owner identity is enforced, but a successful real owner browser sign-in has not been exercised. Publication and existing-domain cutover are not completed; auto-publish stays off. Notifications are non-blocking attempts, not a guaranteed delivery channel. Export/history limits are disclosed. Card/bank fields are absent and likely credentials rejected, but arbitrary sensitive-data recognition cannot be guaranteed. Anti-spam processing temporarily uses network data in memory without retaining it in CRM records.
